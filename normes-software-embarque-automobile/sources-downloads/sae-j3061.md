## Purpose and Scope
SAE J3061 provides guidance on vehicle cybersecurity and was created based on existing practices implemented in industry, government, and conference papers. The best practices are intended to be flexible, pragmatic, and adaptable for the vehicle industry as well as other cyber-physical vehicle systems.

## Key Guiding Principles
1. **Know Your System's Cybersecurity Potential** - Understanding the cybersecurity capabilities and limitations of your system
2. **Understand Key Cybersecurity Principles** - Core cybersecurity concepts and best practices
3. **Consider the Vehicle Owners' Use of the System** - Understanding how end-users will interact with the system
4. **Implement Cybersecurity in Concept and Design Phases** - Early integration of cybersecurity considerations
5. **Implement Cybersecurity in Development & Validation** - Continuous cybersecurity throughout development
6. **Implement Cybersecurity in Incident Response** - Processes for handling security incidents
7. **Cybersecurity Considerations When the Vehicle Owner Changes** - Security during ownership transfer

## Process Framework
The standard defines a complete lifecycle process framework that includes:
- **Overall Management of Cybersecurity** - Organizational cybersecurity culture and processes
- **Concept Phase** - Feature definition and threat analysis
- **Product Development** - System, hardware, and software level development
- **Production, Operation & Service** - Manufacturing and field operations
- **Supporting Processes** - Tools, methods, and documentation

## Relationship with ISO/SAE 21434
**Important:** SAE J3061 was superseded by **ISO/SAE 21434:2021** (Road vehicles — Cybersecurity engineering). The newer standard provides more comprehensive requirements for cybersecurity engineering in road vehicles.

ISO/SAE 21434 cancels and supersedes SAE J3061:2016. Organizations should consider transitioning to ISO/SAE 21434 for new projects as it represents the current state-of-the-art in automotive cybersecurity engineering.
