## iso-27001-4 - Context of the organization

- **Official ID:** iso-27001-4
- **Standard / Version:** ISO/IEC 27001:2022
- **Category:** Clause 4
- **Source reference:** ISO/IEC 27001:2022, clause 4
- **Status:** Verified

- **Description:** The organization shall determine external and internal issues that are relevant to its purpose and that affect its ability to achieve the intended outcome(s) of its information security management system. Understanding the context of the organization is crucial for establishing an effective ISMS that addresses specific needs and risks.

## iso-27001-5 - Leadership

- **Official ID:** iso-27001-5
- **Standard / Version:** ISO/IEC 27001:2022
- **Category:** Clause 5
- **Source reference:** ISO/IEC 27001:2022, clause 5
- **Status:** Verified

- **Description:** Top management shall demonstrate leadership and commitment with respect to the information security management system by ensuring information security policy and objectives are established, communicating the importance of effective information security management, and ensuring the integration of information security management system requirements into the organization's business processes.

## iso-27001-A.8 - Asset management

- **Standard / Version:** ISO/IEC 27001:2022
- **Category:** Annex A.8
- **Source reference:** ISO/IEC 27001:2022, Annex A.8
